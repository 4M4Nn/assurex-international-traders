## Rules for new articles

- Add the article to a file in `src/content/blog/`, register it in `articles`, and set its backlog status to `published`. The sitemap, category pages, pagination and llms.txt update automatically.
- Publication and modification dates must be the real dates.
- Don't name an author or reviewer unless they are real and agree to be named.
- Cite primary sources for regulations, customs, food safety and market data (DGFT, APEDA, Spices Board, PPQS, FSSAI, destination-country authorities, ICC).
- Never publish current prices, export volumes or statistics without a dated source.
